STOMPBOX footswitch wiring

Five momentary footswitches on the Raspberry Pi Zero W GPIO header. Each switch has two wires: one to its GPIO pin, one to ground. No resistors needed (the Pi's internal pull-ups are used).

Notes

  • Pin 1 has the square solder pad, at the end of the header next to the SD-card slot. Pin numbers count in pairs from there: odd pins on the inner row, even pins on the board edge.
  • All ground pins (6, 9, 14, 20, 25, 30, 34, 39) are the same. You can also run one shared ground wire to all five switches.
  • Use momentary (soft-touch, normally open) switches. Each switch is just two lugs, so the orientation doesn't matter.
  • Never wire a switch to the 3.3 V (pins 1, 17) or 5 V (pins 2, 4) pins.
  • Each press toggles its effect on the JME STOMPBOX over Bluetooth MIDI (CC 3 MOD, 4 DELAY, 5 REVERB, 7 EQ, 6 GATE).
